Terms

What a reading is

My readings are Self-Knowledge Tarot: the cards are a mirror for reflection, not a way of predicting the future. A reading is my interpretation of the cards I pull for you, offered for personal reflection, inspiration and entertainment. I don't promise or guarantee any outcome.

Your choices are yours

Anything you decide after a reading is your own decision and your own responsibility.

What I don't read on

Readings are not medical, legal, financial, psychological or any other professional advice, and they don't replace a qualified professional. Please don't ask me questions about:

  • health or medical matters
  • legal matters
  • money, investments or finances

If you're in crisis or need urgent help, please contact a professional or local emergency services instead.

Who readings are for

You must be 18 or older to buy a reading.

How live readings work

  • Readings are sold only while I'm live on Instagram (@brianduffygram) and are done during that live.
  • Prices are shown before you buy: one card $5, two cards $10, three cards $15 (USD).
  • I read in the order purchases come in. Give the Instagram handle you're watching from, and stay on the live for your reading.
  • Readings happen in public on the live, so only share what you're comfortable having others hear.

Payment

Payments are processed through Shopify checkout. Your card statement will show a charge from Brian Duffy Tarot.
